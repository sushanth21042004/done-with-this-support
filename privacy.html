Private by design.

Done With This is built to organize files without collecting your personal data.

Data collection

Done With This does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information. The app has no account system, advertising SDK, analytics SDK, or remote file-processing service.

Files and folder access

The app can access only folders you explicitly choose through the macOS folder picker. File names, metadata, hashes used for duplicate detection, and storage calculations are processed locally on your Mac. File contents are not uploaded.

Cleanup and restoration

Items selected for cleanup are moved to the macOS Trash rather than permanently erased. The app stores cleanup history, exclusions, settings, and security-scoped folder bookmarks locally on your Mac so it can provide restore and automation features.

Purchases

Lifetime Pro purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit. Apple may process purchase and transaction information under Apple’s own privacy policy. Done With This receives only the entitlement status required to unlock Pro.

Changes and questions

This policy may be updated when the app changes. The effective date above will be revised when material changes are published. For privacy questions, use the contact option on the Support page.
