Contact support

Why can’t the app scan a folder?

Choose the folder again using “Change folder…” so macOS can renew the app’s permission.

Are files permanently deleted?

No. Done With This moves selected items to macOS Trash. You can restore them from History or Trash while they remain available.

Why did restore ask for a folder?

macOS may require permission before restoring an older history item. Select its original folder when prompted.

Why isn’t my whole drive categorized?

The total used/free view covers the drive. Detailed categories cover folders you explicitly allow, preserving macOS privacy protections.

How do I restore Pro?

Open Pro and choose “Restore Purchase” while signed into the same Apple Account used for the original purchase.

How does duplicate detection work?

Potential duplicates are matched locally by size and cryptographic file hash. No file contents leave your Mac.
